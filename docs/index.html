The proposition

Work should outlive the model.

AssistOS is a project-centred workspace and ecosystem for agentic computing. It makes intelligence installable, governable, portable, and useful across devices, organizations, and models.

Instead of another isolated chat window, AssistOS keeps the artifacts of work at the center: documents, source code, datasets, decisions, versions, evidence, and the processes that connect them. Agents act through visible permissions, not invisible access.

Explore

One platform. Clear layers.

Home contains the public Vision, technical Whitepaper, and developer resources. Research contains the research foundation, architecture, Wormhole, archive, and the wider ecosystem of AssistOS tools. Each external tool opens on its own focused Research page, inside a framed workspace that preserves the AssistOS context.

Local-first does not mean cloud-free. It means location, access, and continuity remain choices.
