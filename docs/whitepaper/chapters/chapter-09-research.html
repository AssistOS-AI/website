Chapter 9: AI Research Agents

AI Research Agents are specialized agents designed to assist researchers in literature review, hypothesis generation, experimental design, data analysis, and knowledge synthesis.

Coming Soon: This chapter is currently being prepared. Detailed documentation about AI Research Agents' architectures, capabilities, and integration with the AssistOS research workflow will be added in an upcoming update.

Research Context

Built on the foundation of the Achilles research project and Axiologic Research's expertise, these agents are designed to support the full research lifecycle:

  • Literature discovery and summarization
  • Research question formulation and refinement
  • Experimental design assistance
  • Data analysis and visualization
  • Paper writing and bibliography management
  • Peer review preparation

These agents will be documented in detail as they mature through the Achilles project research activities.
