Chapter 12: Data Sharing & Provenance

Data Sharing and Provenance addresses the critical need for tracking the origin, transformation, and sharing of data and artifacts within the AssistOS ecosystem. In a world where AI agents create and modify content, knowing where something came from and how it evolved is essential.

The Provenance Challenge

When AI agents are involved in creating content, traditional notions of authorship and origin become more complex. AssistOS addresses this through:

  • Complete lineage tracking for all artifacts from specification to final output
  • Agent attribution: which agents contributed to which parts of an artifact
  • Model attribution: which AI models were used in the generation process
  • Version-controlled specifications that serve as the "source of truth"
  • Secure sharing mechanisms that preserve provenance information

This framework ensures that every artifact produced by AssistOS carries with it a complete, verifiable history of its creation.
