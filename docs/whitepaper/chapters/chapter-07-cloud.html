Chapter 7: Ploinky Cloud

Ploinky Cloud is the hosted, managed version of the Ploinky platform, designed for users and organizations that want the power of Ploinky without managing infrastructure themselves.

Coming Soon: We are preparing to launch the first version of Ploinky Cloud. This will be a separately licensable offering for organizations interested in hosted Ploinky capabilities.

Vision

While Ploinky itself is open source (MIT License), Ploinky Cloud will provide additional managed services including:

  • Hosted agent environments with automatic scaling
  • Managed infrastructure and security updates
  • Multi-tenant isolation and access control
  • Integrated monitoring and observability dashboards
  • Backup and disaster recovery

Licensing

Ploinky Cloud is not open source and will be available under a separate commercial license. Organizations interested in offering hosted Ploinky services to their own customers can explore licensing opportunities for providing Ploinky hosting.

We will announce launch dates and pricing details as they become available. For early access or licensing inquiries, please contact us.
