Chapter 14: Federated learning architecture

The proposed federated learning architecture lets organizations train a shared model while keeping source datasets in their own workspaces. Local DPU Agents would run approved training tasks and export permitted updates. This is a research design awaiting implementation and validation.

What the design adds

A hospital, company or laboratory may have data it cannot pool with a partner. Federated training moves the computation plan and model updates instead. Keeping source data local helps limit data movement, but updates and the final model can still reveal information.

The Ploinky design combines local encrypted storage, task and export policies, signed transfers and records of each training round.

Components

  • The Coordinator Agent defines the federation, invites participants, distributes rounds and aggregates accepted updates.
  • Each DPU Agent controls its local LightDSU packages and decides which training and exports are allowed.
  • Wormhole transports plans, models, updates and confirmations between workspaces.
  • LightDSU stores encrypted data and provenance records.
  • A Federated Training Skill runs the approved method inside the participant's sandbox.

A training round

  1. The coordinator signs a plan with the model, training skill, aggregation method, participation conditions and security requirements.
  2. Each DPU checks data compatibility, training rights, export policy, installed skills and sandbox capacity. It accepts or refuses locally.
  3. The coordinator supplies the current model. The local skill trains on the permitted package and records the parameters, metrics and output.
  4. The DPU checks the proposed export. Wormhole transfers approved updates through signed, encrypted and confirmed chunks.
  5. The coordinator or Secure Aggregation Agent combines accepted updates. The new model and aggregation history enter the ModelDSU.
  6. The final model undergoes validation and export review before use outside the federation.

Authority stays local

The coordinator may request training; it cannot grant itself access to a participant's data. The training skill sees the allowed data inside its sandbox. Other agents use the DPU interface, and only approved updates and metrics leave.

The Wormhole signaling service should receive connection metadata rather than plaintext content. These boundaries need testing in the full deployment.

Privacy controls

Secure aggregation is intended to prevent the coordinator from inspecting individual contributions. It could be a separate agent or a coordinator mode. Its assumptions and minimum participant requirements need to be explicit.

Additional policies may require gradient clipping, noise, limited metrics, minimum group sizes and leakage tests. The proposed first version includes update clipping, format validation, a ban on individual prediction exports and round logging. Those controls alone do not establish differential privacy.

Provenance

Local records should connect dataset hashes, the input model, training skill, environment, parameters, metrics, update and export decision. Coordinator records should identify included updates, aggregation method, dropouts, output version and checks.

The proposed profiles are AI_ML_EXPERIMENT, LIGHTDSU_MINIMAL and W3C_PROV. They connect datasets and models to the operations and participants that produced them.

The first test is a reproducible federation where participants can inspect and enforce their own participation and export decisions.
