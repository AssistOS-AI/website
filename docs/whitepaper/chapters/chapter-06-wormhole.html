What to Expect

Ploinky Wormhole will address the challenge of connecting AI agents and workspaces across different environments securely. Key capabilities under development include:

  • Secure peer-to-peer connections between Ploinky instances
  • Encrypted communication channels for agent-to-agent interaction
  • Cross-machine workspace synchronization
  • Collaborative agent orchestration across distributed environments
